Profile

Coming from a rural area, ethical science and accessible education have been very important to me.My work spans inorganic chemistry, radio astronomy, searching for life beyond Earth, and science outreach. I am committed to helping everyone follow their curiosities and explore the universe around us.

My path following this has been unusual compared to most. I was drawn to research from a young age, joining the residential collegiate high school Arkansas School for Mathematics, Sciences, and the Arts. This early exposure to research guided me to Lyon College as an Honors Fellow, where I was able to perform the work that led to my first two publications. Despite the excellent chemistry opportunities, Lyon lacked the resources to give me a Physics major. So, I attended the University of Central Arkansas. This is also where I began working in radio astronomy. That work further led me to the SETI Institute, where I have taken an agnostic approach in searching for life beyond Earth. Now, I look to the future of my education and career as I seek to join these passions and attempt to understand how chemistry and physics interact across the universe.
